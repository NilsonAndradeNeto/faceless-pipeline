"Get the suitcase in my closet." Those were Amelia Earhart’s final, desperate words—and she wasn't speaking from the bottom of the ocean. For days after her plane vanished, the world assumed she was dead, but her radio was screaming for help. A listener heard a distressed woman through the static while an injured man shouted that the water was knee-deep. Then came the chilling request: Earhart told her husband to get the suitcase in her California closet. Four years earlier, she’d written to her mother asking that the suitcase be destroyed if anything happened to her. On a deserted island called Nikumaroro, the mystery gets even darker. Searchers found a shoe, a bottle, a sextant box, and a skeleton resting under a tree. A doctor claimed the bones were a man's, but they were later misplaced and lost. But later researchers used those same measurements to argue the bones were not male. She was stranded, watching the horizon for a rescue that never came. If the bones were hers, they were misplaced in Fiji and lost forever.
