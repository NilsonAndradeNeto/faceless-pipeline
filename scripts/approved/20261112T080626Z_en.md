In July 1937, a radio listener heard a terrifying broadcast: a distressed woman beside an injured man yelling, 'Water's knee deep – let me out!' When Amelia Earhart vanished over the Pacific Ocean, the Navy believed her plane ran out of fuel and sank. But for days, home radios picked up frantic distress calls no one was supposed to hear. Betty Klenck listened in horror. She heard a distressed woman crying beside an injured man. Then the woman repeated a strange instruction: 'George, get the suitcase in my closet.' Years before, Amelia asked her mother to destroy a suitcase in her closet. Later, searchers on a desolate island found a woman's shoe heel. Under a tree, they found human bones. A doctor measured the skeleton and claimed it belonged to a male. Then, the bones vanished forever. Were those radio signals Amelia Earhart's final distress cries on that distant reef? What do you believe actually happened to her?
