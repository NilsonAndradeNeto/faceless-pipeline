A crew of 129 men sailed into the freezing Arctic. In 1845, Sir John Franklin led the most advanced ships on Earth to find the Northwest Passage. None of them ever came back. Trapped in the ice near King William Island, the crew waited for more than a year. But terror was already inside their supplies. Eight thousand tin cans of food were sealed with lead solder. As starvation set in, lead poisoning and disease slowly killed them. In 1848, the survivors abandoned the ships and headed south into the white wasteland. They set out for the Canadian mainland, but disappeared into the frozen wilderness. Then came the darkest turn. When scientists finally found their remains, they discovered human bones with clear knife marks on them. To stay alive in the freezing wind, they had eaten each other. We found the ships, but their final moments remain a frozen ghost. If you were starving in total darkness, at what point would you do the unthinkable?
