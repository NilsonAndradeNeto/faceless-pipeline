In the late thirteen hundreds, a cove on Oak Island experienced a massive saltwater intrusion, likely from human manipulation. In 1795, Daniel McInnis spotted a strange pit on an isolated island. He began digging into damp dirt, but at ten feet down, a solid layer of oak logs blocked his path. He dug deeper. Every ten feet, another wooden platform blocked his way. Years later, excavators pushed down to ninety feet. Then the trap sprung. Flooding filled the shaft, halting their work. Near the shore, searchers found strange non-native coconut fibers hidden in the beach. Then, modern scientists tested core samples from the island swamp. Saltwater invaded that basin in the late thirteen hundreds. Analysis of core samples suggests human manipulation altered the swamp long before settlers arrived. This centuries-old obsession led to relentless searches and excavations. What were they protecting beneath the fog and mud? What do you think is down there?
