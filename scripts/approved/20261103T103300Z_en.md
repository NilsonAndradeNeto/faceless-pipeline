In 1590, John White stepped onto a dark beach, desperate to find his baby granddaughter. Three years earlier, he had left his daughter, baby Virginia Dare, and English settlers on Roanoke Island. He promised to return quickly with supplies, but a sudden war trapped him in England for three grueling years. Now, night was falling as his boat finally touched the sand. He called into the pitch-black trees. Nothing. Silence. The entire fortified settlement was completely empty. The settlers had simply ceased to exist. A fully built, heavily protected village had vanished without a trace. As White searched the abandoned fort, he found only one chilling clue. Carved deep into a wooden post was a single mysterious word: CROATOAN. Before he could search the nearby island, violent storms snapped his anchors and blew his ships out to sea, ensuring he would never see his family again.
